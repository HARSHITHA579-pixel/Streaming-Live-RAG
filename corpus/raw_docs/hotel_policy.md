# Corporate Lodging and Hotel Policy

## Preferred Lodging and Booking Channels
The Corporate Lodging and Hotel Policy establishes standards for lodging quality, safety, cost limits, and booking channels for business trips:
- **Mandatory Booking Channel:** All individual and small-group hotel reservations must be booked directly through the Enterprise Travel Portal or the authorized corporate travel management agency. 
- **Direct Bookings:** Hotel accommodations booked directly on third-party consumer booking websites (e.g., Expedia, Booking.com, Airbnb) are non-compliant and will not be reimbursed unless an official exception waiver is granted in writing by the Corporate Travel Desk prior to the stay.
- **Corporate Rate Agreements:** Employees are required to select preferred corporate hotel chains (e.g., Marriott, Hyatt, Hilton, IHG) where discounted corporate negotiated rates and complimentary amenities (such as breakfast or Wi-Fi) are automatically applied.

## Nightly Room Rate Caps and Tiered Ceilings
Reimbursement for hotel accommodations is subject to nightly room rate caps determined by geographic destination tiers. Nightly caps apply to the base room rate excluding mandatory local occupancy taxes and tourism fees:
- **Standard Domestic Locations:** The maximum allowable base rate is $220 per night.
- **Tier 1 Domestic Metropolitan Areas:** In high-cost urban centers (including New York City, San Francisco, Boston, Washington D.C., and Chicago), the maximum allowable base rate is $320 per night.
- **International Standard Destinations:** The maximum allowable base rate is $260 per night.
- **International High-Cost Tier 1 Metros:** For major global financial hubs (including London, Tokyo, Paris, Geneva, Zurich, and Singapore), the nightly room cap is $350 per night.
- **Conference Room Blocks:** If an employee is attending an approved corporate conference or industry convention with an official designated conference room block rate that exceeds the standard tier ceiling, the higher rate is approved automatically provided the reservation is confirmed within the official block.

## Room Categories and Allowable Amenities
Employees must select standard single-occupancy accommodations when booking business travel lodging:
- **Eligible Room Types:** Standard Queen or King single-occupancy rooms are authorized. Suite upgrades, executive level lounges, penthouse suites, and ocean-view premium rooms are not reimbursable unless provided at zero additional cost by the hotel.
- **Complimentary Services and Internet:** High-speed internet access for business work is fully reimbursable if not provided complimentary by the hotel.
- **Fitness and Wellness Facilities:** Standard complimentary access to hotel fitness centers is expected; paid premium fitness classes or spa access are personal expenses.
- **Room Service:** In-room dining and room service are permitted only if total meal costs fall within the traveler's daily per diem allowance ($85/day domestic, $125/day Tier 1 international).

## Check-In, Incidentals, and Personal Charges
Hotels require a valid payment method upon check-in to secure incidental charges:
- **Personal Card for Incidentals:** Employees must provide a personal credit card at check-in to cover all non-business incidental charges.
- **Non-Reimbursable Incidentals:** In-room mini-bar items, pay-per-view movies, video games, dry cleaning for stays under 5 nights, and discretionary personal services are not reimbursable.
- **Extended Stay Laundry:** For continuous business stays exceeding 5 consecutive nights at a single hotel location, reasonable laundry and pressing charges are reimbursable up to a maximum of $30 per calendar week.

## Hotel Cancellation Windows and No-Show Fees
To prevent unnecessary financial penalties from unused hotel bookings, employees must adhere to hotel cancellation rules:
- **Cancellation Deadline:** Hotel reservations must be cancelled at least 48 hours prior to the standard local check-in time (typically 3:00 PM local time on day of arrival), or earlier if specified by a specific property's contract.
- **No-Show Penalties:** If a traveler fails to check in and did not cancel within the permitted cancellation window, the hotel will charge a one-night room and tax penalty fee.
- **Liability for Penalties:** No-show penalty fees resulting from employee oversight or voluntary schedule adjustments without business justification are the personal responsibility of the employee and will not be reimbursed. If a cancellation is caused by unavoidable business emergencies or documented flight cancellations, the penalty fee is reimbursable with Department Head approval.
