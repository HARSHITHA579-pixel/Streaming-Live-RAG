# International Travel Policy

## Scope and Pre-Approval Requirements
The International Travel Policy governs all employee business travel originating in or extending to countries outside the employee's designated home operating territory. Due to elevated logistics, cost, and safety considerations, all international trips require rigorous pre-approval workflows:
- **Approval Hierarchy:** International itineraries must receive written pre-approval from both the employee's Department Head and the respective Vice President (VP) at least 30 calendar days prior to the planned departure date.
- **Global Security and Risk Assessment:** Before any international booking can be ticketed, the itinerary must undergo a mandatory security screening by the Global Corporate Security Office. Travel to countries flagged as high-risk by government advisory levels requires additional sign-off from the Chief Operating Officer (COO).
- **Early Planning Windows:** Last-minute international travel requests submitted with fewer than 14 days notice require executive justification detailing why the trip could not be scheduled in advance.

## International Flight Class Tiers
Flight class eligibility for international routes is determined by scheduled continuous air travel time (excluding airport layovers):
- **Short-to-Medium International Flights (Under 7 Hours):** All international flight segments under 7 hours continuous flying time must be booked in Standard Economy class.
- **Long-Haul International Flights (7 Hours and Above):** For uninterrupted one-way flight segments exceeding 7 continuous flight hours, employees are permitted to book Business Class or Premium Economy. This applies to cross-oceanic itineraries to ensure adequate rest and readiness for business duties upon arrival.
- **First Class Restrictions:** First Class air travel is strictly prohibited for all employees, regardless of job title, seniority, or flight duration.
- **Rest Days After Arrival:** For international flights involving a time zone transition of 6 or more hours, employees are permitted one non-working rest day upon arrival prior to commencing business meetings. Hotel accommodations for this rest day are fully covered.

## Passport, Visa, and Immunization Expenses
The company reimburses employees for administrative costs directly associated with securing necessary documentation for authorized international business assignments:
- **Visa Fees:** Business visa application fees, consulate appointment charges, biometric processing fees, and mandatory international entry permits are 100% reimbursable upon submission of valid receipts.
- **Passport Renewals:** Passport renewal fees are reimbursable if the traveler is required to travel internationally on behalf of the company and has fewer than 6 months of validity remaining on their current passport, or if additional blank visa pages are required.
- **Required Immunizations and Medications:** Mandatory vaccinations, preventative immunizations (e.g., Yellow Fever, Typhoid), and prescription travel prophylaxis (e.g., anti-malarial medication) recommended by health authorities for the destination country are fully covered.
- **Expedited Services:** Expedited courier and consular processing fees are reimbursable only if the travel request was initiated on an urgent basis at the express request of company leadership.

## International Per Diem and Currency Conversion
Daily living allowances for international assignments are adjusted based on destination city cost tiers:
- **Tier 1 International Metros:** For high-cost global metropolitan centers (e.g., London, Tokyo, Zurich, Singapore, New York), the international daily meal allowance is $125 per day ($30 breakfast, $40 lunch, $55 dinner).
- **Tier 2 International Cities:** For all standard international destinations not designated as Tier 1, the international daily meal allowance is $95 per day ($25 breakfast, $30 lunch, $40 dinner).
- **Foreign Exchange Fees:** Mandatory bank conversion fees, credit card international transaction fees (typically 1% to 3%), and currency exchange booth service charges are fully reimbursable when supported by statements.
- **Exchange Rate Application:** Expenses incurred in foreign currencies must be converted to local home currency using the historical exchange rate on the date of transaction from the Enterprise Finance Portal or the traveler's official credit card statement.

## Travel Emergency and Medical Evacuation
Employee health, safety, and security are paramount during overseas assignments:
- **Corporate Medical Coverage:** All international travelers are automatically enrolled in the Corporate International SOS medical and emergency assistance program. This policy covers emergency medical treatment, hospitalization, prescription replacement, and medical evacuation to the nearest accredited medical center.
- **Traveler Tracking Registry:** Prior to departure, employees must register their complete flight itinerary, hotel address, and active local contact number in the Global Traveler Registry.
- **Emergency Incident Protocol:** In the event of political unrest, natural disasters, or severe health emergencies, travelers must immediately contact the 24/7 Global Security Operations Hotline and follow instructed evacuation procedures.
