# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submission Timelines and Deadlines
To maintain accurate financial accounting and timely budget reconciliations, all employees must submit expense reimbursement claims promptly:
- **Standard Submission Window:** Expense claims must be submitted via the Enterprise Finance Management portal within 21 calendar days following the conclusion of the business trip or event date.
- **Monthly Recurring Expenses:** Continuous expenses (e.g., local transit passes, mobile phone allowances) must be submitted by the 5th business day of the following calendar month.
- **Late Submission Grace Period:** Expense reports submitted between 22 and 45 calendar days after the event require written justification and will be subject to a secondary review by the Department Finance Manager.
- **Forfeiture of Reimbursement:** Any expense report submitted more than 45 calendar days after the completion of travel is deemed expired and will be automatically rejected, forfeiting reimbursement eligibility, unless an executive exemption is granted by the VP of Finance.

## Receipt Requirements and Documentation Thresholds
Comprehensive documentation is required for all expense claims submitted to Corporate Accounts Payable:
- **Itemized Receipts:** Itemized digital receipts showing the date, vendor name, specific items purchased, unit cost, taxes, and total amount are required for any single transaction exceeding $25. Credit card charge slips showing only the final total are not sufficient without the accompanying itemized receipt.
- **Threshold for Missing Receipts:** For out-of-pocket expenses under $75 where obtaining a physical receipt was not feasible (such as coin-operated parking or small cash tips), an electronic Missing Receipt Declaration Form may be submitted. An employee may utilize this exception a maximum of 3 times per calendar year.
- **Digital Image Quality:** All uploaded receipts must be clear, fully legible, and properly rotated. Blurred, clipped, or unreadable receipts will delay claim approval.
- **Pre-Paid Corporate Invoices:** Expenses billed directly to corporate master accounts (such as company-arranged group venue deposits) must not be submitted on individual employee expense reports.

## Approval Workflow and Thresholds
All reimbursement claims are routed dynamically through an approval hierarchy based on the cumulative financial total:
- **Tier 1 (Claims up to $1,000):** Requires direct manager electronic sign-off. Approvals are typically processed within 3 business days of submission.
- **Tier 2 (Claims from $1,001 to $5,000):** Requires approval from both the Direct Manager and the Department Head / Director.
- **Tier 3 (Claims exceeding $5,000):** Requires approval from the Direct Manager, Department Head, VP of Business Unit, and the Corporate Finance Controller.
- **Out-of-Policy Exceptions:** Any expense claim containing line items exceeding established policy caps (such as hotel nightly room ceilings or meal per diem limits) automatically triggers an Out-of-Policy audit flag requiring explicit justification and VP sign-off before payment.

## Foreign Currency and Exchange Calculations
For expenses incurred during international travel, the following currency conversion rules apply:
- **Credit Card Billing Statements:** If transactions were paid using a personal or corporate credit card, the employee must attach the corresponding monthly credit card statement showing the exact converted local currency amount and foreign transaction fee.
- **Cash Conversions:** If cash was converted into local currency abroad, the currency exchange receipt must be uploaded to substantiate the conversion rate.
- **Standard Spot Rate Fallback:** In the absence of an exchange receipt or card statement conversion, the Finance Portal automatically calculates exchange rates using the official OANDA or European Central Bank benchmark rate on the transaction date.

## Payment Timelines and Disputed Claims
Corporate Accounts Payable processes approved reimbursement batches according to the standard payroll and payment cycle:
- **Disbursement Schedule:** Once an expense report receives all required managerial approvals, payment is issued via direct deposit within 10 business days (or on the next regular bi-weekly payroll cycle).
- **Disputed Line Items:** If an auditor or approver rejects a specific line item on an expense report, the disputed line is separated into an inquiry queue. The undisputed balance of the claim is approved and paid on schedule, preventing full claim delays.
- **Overpayments and Audits:** The company reserves the right to audit expense reports for up to 24 months post-reimbursement. Any accidental duplicate payment or fraudulent claim will be recovered via payroll adjustment.
