# Corporate Booking and Reservation Policy

## Centralized Booking System and Self-Service
The Corporate Booking and Reservation Policy defines the mandatory procedures for reserving business travel, transportation, accommodations, and meeting spaces across the enterprise:
- **Mandatory Self-Service Portal:** All individual business travel reservations—including commercial flights, passenger rail tickets, rental vehicles, and individual hotel rooms—must be booked directly through the Enterprise Travel Portal (powered by Concur/SAP).
- **Prohibition of Direct External Bookings:** Direct reservations made via consumer travel websites, airline portals, online travel agencies (OTAs), or hotel front desks are strictly non-compliant. Bookings made outside the enterprise system will not be reimbursed without a written exception waiver granted by the Corporate Travel Desk.
- **Corporate Credit Card Integration:** Bookings executed within the portal are automatically charged to central corporate billing accounts or designated corporate travel cards, streamlining reconciliation and eliminating employee out-of-pocket exposure.

## Advance Reservation Windows
To ensure optimal pricing and inventory availability, reservations must adhere to minimum advance booking windows:
- **Domestic Travel:** Domestic flights, passenger rail, and hotel accommodations must be booked at least 14 calendar days prior to the date of travel.
- **International Travel:** All international flights and overseas lodging reservations must be booked at least 30 calendar days prior to the departure date to accommodate necessary visa and security approvals.
- **Group Bookings and Room Blocks:** Group reservations involving 10 or more employees or meeting attendees must be initiated at least 45 calendar days in advance.
- **Late Booking Justification:** Any reservation initiated inside the mandatory window triggers an automated notification to the Department Head requiring business justification for the short-notice booking.

## Pre-Approval Thresholds and Authorization Matrix
The booking system enforces tiered authorization rules based on total estimated reservation cost and policy compliance:
- **Tier 1 (Automated Instant Confirmation):** In-policy bookings under $1,500 total value are automatically confirmed and ticketed upon submission without requiring manual manager intervention.
- **Tier 2 (Manager Approval Required):** Bookings between $1,500 and $4,000 total value require electronic sign-off from the traveler's Direct Manager before tickets or room reservations are finalized. Approvals must be completed within 24 hours to prevent airfare expiration.
- **Tier 3 (Executive VP Approval):** Bookings exceeding $4,000, or any booking that includes out-of-policy selections (e.g., flight class upgrades, premium hotel suites, or bookings outside preferred carrier agreements), require formal authorization from the Business Unit VP.
- **Unapproved Holds:** Flight fares placed on temporary reservation hold that do not receive necessary manager sign-off within 24 hours will automatically lapse and be cancelled by the system.

## Group Bookings and Multi-Attendee Coordination
Specialized coordination protocols apply when multiple company personnel attend the same event, customer summit, or team workshop:
- **Group Travel Desk Threshold:** When 10 or more employees are traveling to the same destination for the same business purpose or corporate event, reservations must NOT be made as individual portal bookings. Organizers must submit a Group Travel Request to the Corporate Group Travel Desk.
- **Negotiated Block Rates:** The Group Travel Desk negotiates collective group rates, dedicated hotel room blocks, consolidated ground transfers, and group airline ticketing discounts.
- **Flight Dispersion Rule:** For operational risk mitigation, no more than 15 company employees (and no more than 3 members of the Executive Leadership Team) may travel on the exact same scheduled commercial flight segment.

## Reservation Modification and Rebooking Rules
Procedures for adjusting existing itineraries after initial ticketing and confirmation:
- **Permitted Modifications:** Itinerary modifications (such as changing travel dates, flight times, or hotel checkout dates) are permitted when driven by genuine business requirements, meeting postponements, or client schedule shifts.
- **Employee-Initiated Personal Changes:** Any modification requested for personal convenience is subject to applicable airline or hotel change fees and fare differentials, which must be paid directly by the employee.
- **Modification Channel:** All reservation changes must be executed through the Enterprise Travel Portal or via the 24/7 Corporate Travel Agent Desk. Modifying tickets directly at airline ticket counters without Travel Desk notification is prohibited as it causes corporate tracking and itinerary synchronization errors.
