# Travel and Event Cancellation Policy

## Scope and Authority to Cancel
The Travel and Event Cancellation Policy outlines the procedures, liabilities, and financial implications of cancelling authorized business travel, external lodging, catering arrangements, and corporate events:
- **Authority to Cancel:** Any decision to cancel or substantially modify an approved corporate trip or organized event must be authorized in writing by the original approving manager or event sponsor.
- **Duty to Minimize Financial Impact:** Travelers and event organizers have an affirmative duty to cancel non-essential services immediately upon learning of a change in schedule to avoid penalty fees and forfeit deadlines.
- **Reporting Cancellations:** All cancellations must be processed through the Enterprise Travel Portal or Corporate Event Management Desk, rather than contacting third-party service providers directly, to ensure corporate credits and refund receipts are properly logged.

## Flight Cancellation and Airline Credit Rules
When business flights are cancelled, tickets are handled according to fare class terms and airline contract rules:
- **Non-Refundable Ticket Credits:** Most corporate airfare is booked under non-refundable standard fares. When such flights are cancelled prior to scheduled departure, the remaining value is converted into an airline electronic travel credit (e-credit).
- **Ownership of Credits:** All airline ticket credits generated from company-funded travel remain the exclusive property of the company. E-credits must be applied toward future business travel for the same employee within 12 months of the original issue date.
- **Ticket Expiration:** Unused airline credits cannot be cashed out or transferred to personal accounts. If an employee leaves the company, unutilized credits are surrendered to the corporate travel pool where permitted by airline rules.
- **Rebooking and Change Fees:** Change fees incurred due to bona fide business necessity or unexpected corporate restructuring are fully reimbursable. Change fees incurred due to personal convenience or voluntary schedule alterations are the responsibility of the traveler.

## Hotel and Ground Transit Cancellation Rules
Lodging and ground transportation bookings must be cancelled promptly in accordance with vendor terms:
- **Hotel Notice Period:** Hotel reservations must be cancelled at least 48 hours prior to local check-in time (or the specific cancellation cutoff indicated on the reservation confirmation).
- **Late Hotel Cancellations:** Cancellations made within the 48-hour window or failure to appear (no-show) incur a penalty charge equal to one night's room and tax. This fee is reimbursable only if caused by medical emergencies, severe weather disruptions, or client-initiated meeting cancellations verified by the traveler's manager.
- **Rental Cars:** Rental vehicle reservations through corporate partner agencies may be cancelled up to 24 hours prior to pickup without financial penalty. Same-day cancellations may incur a nominal administrative fee of $25.
- **Rail Tickets:** Amtrak and regional rail tickets must be cancelled at least 1 hour prior to departure to receive full refund or travel credit.

## Event Venue and Group Booking Penalties
Cancelling off-site corporate events, workshops, team off-sites, and venue reservations involves substantial contractual obligations:
- **60+ Days Prior to Event:** Cancellations submitted 60 calendar days or more prior to the event start date incur a 10% administrative contract processing fee. The remainder of any advanced deposit will be refunded to the corporate account.
- **30 to 59 Days Prior to Event:** Cancellations made between 30 and 59 calendar days prior forfeit the initial 50% contract deposit.
- **Fewer than 30 Days Prior to Event:** Cancellations made fewer than 30 calendar days before the event start date result in forfeiture of 100% of the total venue contract value.
- **Force Majeure:** Cancellation penalties are waived only under formal force majeure conditions (e.g., natural disasters, government-mandated travel bans, state emergencies) as outlined in the executed vendor contract.

## Catering and Food Service Cancellation Schedules
Catering orders for meetings and events carry strict preparation and sourcing deadlines:
- **Full Refund Window:** Catering orders cancelled at least 5 full business days prior to the event date receive a 100% refund of all catering fees.
- **Partial Refund Window:** Cancellations made between 48 hours and 5 business days prior to the scheduled service incur a 50% charge of the total estimated food and beverage order.
- **Late Cancellations (Under 48 Hours):** Catering orders cancelled with fewer than 48 hours notice require 100% payment of the full order total, as food preparation and ingredient procurement will have already commenced.
