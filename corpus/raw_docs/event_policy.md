# Corporate Event and Conference Policy

## Event Planning Timelines and Lead Times
The Corporate Event and Conference Policy sets out requirements for initiating, budgeting, planning, and executing internal and external corporate events, customer summits, team off-sites, and technical workshops:
- **Small Internal Events (Up to 25 Attendees):** Planning must begin at least 30 calendar days prior to the event date. Requires Department Manager approval.
- **Medium Events & Workshops (26 to 100 Attendees):** Requires a minimum of 60 calendar days planning lead time, approval from the Business Unit Director, and formal event registration with the Corporate Events Team.
- **Major Conferences & International Summits (Over 100 Attendees):** Requires at least 120 calendar days lead time, detailed financial modeling, and formal review and authorization by the Executive Event Review Board (ERB).
- **Short-Notice Events:** Any event requested with less than the required lead time requires exceptional approval from the Chief Operating Officer (COO) and is subject to availability of venue resources.

## Venue Sourcing and Contract Execution
To optimize corporate expenditure and mitigate legal risks associated with physical venue bookings:
- **Internal Facility First Principle:** Before contracting an external commercial venue, event organizers must check internal company auditorium and conference facilities. External venue booking will only be approved if internal facilities are unavailable or inadequate for the event scope.
- **Venue Contract Execution:** Event organizers are not legally authorized to sign venue agreements, banquet event orders (BEOs), or hotel master service agreements. All contracts with a financial commitment exceeding $2,500 must be reviewed by Corporate Legal and signed exclusively by an authorized representative from Corporate Procurement.
- **Site Visits:** Pre-event site inspections are reimbursable only for events exceeding 75 attendees and are limited to a single traveling planner for a maximum of 2 days.

## Attendee Limits and Guest Registration
Managing guest lists and participant credentials is vital for physical security and compliance:
- **Employee-to-Guest Ratio:** For recruitment, networking, or client-focused events, an employee-to-external guest ratio of at least 1 employee per 3 external guests (1:3) must be maintained to maximize business impact.
- **Guest List Deadlines:** All external guest lists, speaker credentials, and participant rosters must be finalized and submitted to Corporate Security at least 5 business days prior to the event for security badging and visitor clearance.
- **Unregistered Attendees:** Walk-in or unregistered attendees will not be granted access to closed corporate events or workshops without on-site authorization from the Lead Event Sponsor.

## Budgeting, Deposit Schedules, and Expense Tracking
Corporate events must operate strictly within approved line-item budget allocations:
- **Budget Approval:** A comprehensive event budget outlining venue rental, audio/visual (AV) production, catering, marketing collateral, speaker honorariums, and attendee transportation must be approved prior to making financial commitments.
- **Standard Deposit Milestones:** Venue contract payments must follow standard corporate milestone schedules: an initial deposit capped at 25% upon contract execution, a secondary 50% progress payment 30 calendar days before the event, and the final 25% balance paid within 30 days post-event upon satisfactory delivery of services.
- **Budget Overruns:** Any projected budget variance exceeding 10% of the total approved budget requires immediate resubmission and approval from the Business Unit VP.

## Safety, Accessibility, and Emergency Protocols
Ensuring the safety, accessibility, and well-being of all participants is mandatory:
- **ADA Accessibility Compliance:** All external venues must be fully compliant with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) or equivalent local accessibility statutes, including wheelchair ramps, accessible restrooms, and hearing-assistive AV technology upon request.
- **Emergency Action Plans:** Event organizers must obtain and review the venue's emergency evacuation plans, assembly zones, and medical response procedures prior to the event start.
- **On-Site First Aid:** For all corporate events exceeding 50 in-person attendees, a certified on-site first aid coordinator or venue emergency medical responder must be designated and available during all event hours.
