# Corporate Catering and Dining Policy

## Catering Eligibility and Meal Occasions
The Corporate Catering and Dining Policy provides financial guidelines and operational rules for providing food and beverages during company-sponsored meetings, training sessions, workshops, client entertainment, and corporate events:
- **Eligible Occasions:** Internal catering may be ordered only for business meetings lasting 4 continuous hours or longer, full-day training workshops, cross-departmental summits, or meetings where working through lunch is essential to operational objectives. Routine team meetings under 4 hours are not eligible for catered meals.
- **Client and External Guest Meetings:** Catering is fully permitted for meetings involving external clients, partners, candidates, or vendor negotiations, regardless of meeting length.
- **Pre-Approval Requirements:** All catering orders must receive written pre-approval from the host Department Manager at least 7 business days prior to the event date. Orders exceeding $1,000 require secondary approval from the Department Director.

## Per-Person Spending Caps
To ensure budgetary consistency and fiscal responsibility across departments, all catered food and beverage orders must adhere to fixed per-person spending caps. Caps include food, non-alcoholic beverages, delivery fees, and service gratuities:
- **Breakfast / Morning Refreshments:** Maximum $25 per person (e.g., continental breakfast, pastries, fruit, coffee/tea service).
- **Working Lunch / Boxed Lunch:** Maximum $35 per person (e.g., gourmet sandwiches, salads, buffet lunch, hot working lunch).
- **Afternoon Snack Break:** Maximum $15 per person (e.g., light snacks, baked goods, bottled sparkling water).
- **Casual Evening Reception / Team Dinner:** Maximum $65 per person (e.g., buffet dinner, heavy hors d'oeuvres).
- **Executive & Formal Client Dinners:** Maximum $110 per person. Dinners exceeding this cap require written authorization from the Business Unit VP.

## Approved Caterers and Vendor Contracting
To ensure food hygiene, quality, and streamlined payment processing:
- **Corporate Preferred Vendor List:** Catering orders exceeding $500 must be placed with approved corporate caterers listed in the Enterprise Procurement Portal. Preferred caterers offer direct corporate master billing, eliminating the need for out-of-pocket employee expense reimbursement.
- **External Unapproved Caterers:** If an unapproved external caterer or restaurant is used due to specialty cuisine or geographic necessity, the organizer must obtain a valid Certificate of Commercial General Liability Insurance and Food Safety Compliance from the vendor at least 10 business days prior to the event.
- **Direct Invoicing:** Invoices from approved caterers are submitted directly to Corporate Accounts Payable and must include the official event reservation ID and approved purchase order (PO) number.

## Dietary Requirements and Waste Reduction
Event organizers are responsible for accommodating diverse attendee dietary needs and avoiding excessive food waste:
- **Dietary Information Collection:** Organizers must collect dietary preferences, food allergies (e.g., gluten-free, nut-free, dairy-free), and religious dietary requirements (e.g., Halal, Kosher) at least 7 calendar days prior to the event.
- **Plant-Based Baseline:** For any group catering order exceeding 15 attendees, a minimum of 20% of all ordered meals must be vegetarian or vegan options.
- **Final Headcount Lock:** The final attendee headcount and dietary breakdown must be confirmed with the caterer exactly 72 hours (3 business days) before the event. Additions within 72 hours are subject to caterer surcharge; reductions after 72 hours do not lower the invoice cost.

## Alcohol Service Guidelines and Approvals
The provision of alcohol at company-sponsored events is subject to strict corporate oversight:
- **Permitted Events:** Alcoholic beverages are permitted only at evening social events, celebration dinners, and formal client entertainment taking place after 5:00 PM local time. Alcohol is strictly prohibited during daytime meetings and working lunch sessions.
- **VP Approval Required:** Serving alcohol at any company event requires written approval from the Business Unit VP and Corporate Human Resources.
- **Service Controls:** Alcohol service must be managed by a licensed and insured third-party bartender or banquet staff. Self-service alcohol stations, open punch bowls, and kegs are strictly prohibited. A 2-drink ticket limit per attendee or a 2-hour maximum open bar service limit is enforced for all company events.
