# Corporate Travel Policy

## General Eligibility and Purpose
The purpose of the Corporate Travel Policy is to establish clear guidelines for all employees undertaking authorized business travel on behalf of the company. Business travel is defined as any journey required for official company operations, client engagements, conferences, or off-site corporate events. All employees, contractors, and authorized representatives are eligible for travel support provided their itinerary is pre-approved by their direct manager. Travel arrangements must balance cost-effectiveness, employee safety, and operational necessity. Pre-approval must be requested and documented through the Enterprise Travel Portal at least 14 calendar days prior to domestic travel departure. Unapproved travel bookings will not be reimbursed and may result in disciplinary action.

## Domestic Air Travel Guidelines
All domestic air travel must be booked in Standard Economy class across all regular routes. Employees are expected to choose the most economical non-stop or reasonable single-stop flight option available within a 2-hour window of their desired departure time. 
- **Flight Duration Exceptions:** For continuous domestic flight segments exceeding 5 hours in duration, employees may select Premium Economy seating with written pre-approval from their Department Head.
- **Preferred Carriers:** Bookings should prioritize approved corporate airline partners unless an alternate carrier offers a fare reduction of 20% or greater for identical routing.
- **Baggage Allowances:** The company reimburses the fee for one standard checked bag per flight. Excess baggage fees, overweight bag charges, and priority boarding fees are not reimbursable unless transporting specialized company equipment authorized in advance.

## Ground Transportation and Rail
Employees traveling domestically are encouraged to utilize convenient and cost-effective ground transit options:
- **Rail Travel:** Standard coach or economy rail travel (such as Amtrak Regional) is fully reimbursable for short-to-medium distance corridors. Business class rail is permitted only on express high-speed services (e.g., Acela) when standard coach is sold out or when travel time is reduced by at least 90 minutes.
- **Rideshare and Taxis:** Ridesharing services (standard Uber, Lyft) and licensed metered taxis are reimbursable for transit between airports, hotels, client sites, and corporate offices. Premium or luxury vehicle tiers (e.g., Uber Black) are strictly non-reimbursable. Daily local rideshare expenses must not exceed $75 per day without prior manager approval.
- **Personal Vehicle Usage:** When an employee drives a personal vehicle for business purposes, reimbursement is paid at the standard corporate rate of $0.67 per mile. This rate covers fuel, wear and tear, and insurance. Fuel receipts for personal vehicles cannot be claimed separately.
- **Rental Cars:** Rental vehicles are permitted only when public transit or ridesharing is impractical or more expensive. The standard authorized vehicle category is Intermediate / Compact Sedan. Upgrades to SUVs or minivans require prior approval and must accommodate three or more traveling colleagues or substantial cargo.

## Per Diem and Daily Meal Allowances
Business travelers are eligible for a daily meal allowance intended to cover reasonable food and beverage costs incurred during travel:
- **Standard Domestic Per Diem:** The standard domestic daily meal allowance is $85 per day, allocated as follows: Breakfast ($20), Lunch ($25), and Dinner ($40).
- **Receipt Submission:** Travelers claiming the per diem rate do not need to provide itemized receipts for individual meals under $25, but total daily expenses must remain within the $85 threshold. Any single meal expense exceeding $50 requires an itemized digital receipt.
- **Single-Day Travel:** Travel that does not include an overnight stay is not eligible for full per diem; only lunch up to $25 may be reimbursed if the workday exceeds 8 hours outside the home office.
- **Alcoholic Beverages:** Personal alcoholic beverages are not eligible for reimbursement under standard travel per diem. Moderate alcohol expenses may only be claimed during approved client entertainment meals in compliance with corporate entertainment guidelines.

## Non-Reimbursable Travel Expenses
The company will not reimburse personal expenses incurred during business trips. Non-reimbursable items include, but are not limited to:
- Traffic citations, parking violation fines, and toll road penalties resulting from driver negligence.
- Airline seat selection upgrade fees, in-flight Wi-Fi for personal use, in-flight entertainment, and lounge day passes.
- Personal side-trips, extended vacation days before or after business travel, and travel expenses for spouses, family members, or personal companions.
- Personal grooming, spa services, fitness center day passes, hotel pay-per-view movies, mini-bar refreshments, and hotel dry cleaning for trips lasting fewer than 5 consecutive days.
- Lost, damaged, or stolen personal belongings (employees should rely on personal or credit card travel insurance).
